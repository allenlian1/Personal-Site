Lego-FIKS is a project we built for the Gen-AI Genesis hackathon, winning best use of AI for generative art. Lego-FIKS revolutionizes autism therapy by transforming children's drawings into interactive 3D LEGO builds designed for behavioral exercises. Built to address the limitations of traditional Applied Behavioral Analysis (ABA), our platform generates layer-by-layer LEGO instructions that assign social roles (guide, supplier, builder) to encourage meaningful peer interaction and reduce therapy-related stress. Using Gemini AI and Shap-E for 3D model generation, the system converts 2D images into voxelized LEGO structures with custom brick-placement algorithms that ensure structural integrity. This gamified approach to social competence therapy fosters authentic communication and self-regulation while preserving the unique qualities that make each child special. By scaling clinically-studied LEGO-based behavioral therapy, we're creating accessible tools that maximize creativity and independence for neuro-divergent children.
